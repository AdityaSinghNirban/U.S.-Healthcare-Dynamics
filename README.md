# 🏥 U.S. Healthcare Dynamics: An In-depth Analysis (2019-2020)
## 📌 Objective
To provide valuable insights to healthcare stakeholders by deciphering complex trends and patterns, empowering decision-makers, healthcare professionals, and policymakers to enhance the overall efficiency and effectiveness of the U.S. healthcare system.
## 🛠️ Tools Used

Power BI
DAX
Excel

## 📊 Dashboards Built
1. Executive Summary

High-level overview of pivotal trends and metrics shaping U.S. healthcare (2019-2020)

2. Hospital Analysis

Performance metrics, patient outcomes & operational efficiency of healthcare institutions

3. Patient Analysis

Demographic trends, treatment effectiveness & overall patient experience

4. Payer-Provider Analysis

Financial interactions, reimbursement trends & payer-provider dynamics

5. Monthly Expenses Overview

Comprehensive snapshot of monthly expenses across the healthcare system
